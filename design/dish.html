National treasure

Plov

More than a dish — a ritual of hospitality. Rice, lamb, carrots and onions slow-cooked in a cast-iron cauldron over an open flame, shared from a single platter.

Origin: Tashkent & Samarkand 2–3 hours Rice · Lamb · Carrot

The story

Plov has been prepared in Uzbekistan for centuries. Each region has its own recipe, and every family guards its version. It is the dish of celebrations, weddings and Friday gatherings — a symbol of generosity and togetherness.
