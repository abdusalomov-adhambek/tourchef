# TOURCHEF — COMPLETE PROJECT DEVELOPMENT PLAN

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Project Name:** TourChef
**Project Type:** Culinary Tourism Marketplace
**Market:** Uzbekistan
**Platform:** Web Application
**Development Approach:** Full-stack development

### Project Purpose

TourChef is a culinary tourism platform that connects tourists with local restaurants, chefs, and authentic Uzbek cooking experiences.

Users can discover traditional Uzbek dishes, watch short cooking and restaurant experience videos, explore culinary masterclasses, book sessions, make payments, attend experiences, and leave reviews.

Restaurants can register on the platform, create their profiles, publish culinary masterclasses, schedule sessions, manage bookings, and track their revenue.

The platform administrator manages users, restaurants, content, bookings, payments, reviews, and the overall platform.

The platform must be developed as a fully functional application, not just a static UI prototype.

# 9. BOOKING AND PAYMENT SYSTEM

Implement a complete booking workflow.

## 9.1 Booking Flow

1. User selects a masterclass.
2. User selects an available session.
3. User selects the number of guests.
4. User enters guest information.
5. System checks available capacity.
6. System temporarily reserves seats.
7. User reviews the price.
8. User proceeds to payment.
9. Payment provider processes the transaction.
10. Payment confirmation is verified.
11. Booking becomes confirmed.
12. User receives confirmation and notification.

## 9.2 Booking Statuses

- Pending Payment
- Confirmed
- Cancelled
- Completed
- Refunded
- No-show

## 9.3 Payment Statuses

- Pending
- Processing
- Paid
- Failed
- Refund Pending
- Refunded

## 9.4 Booking Rules

- Prevent overbooking.
- Use database transactions and row-level locking where necessary.
- Use temporary reservations for unpaid bookings.
- Release expired reservations automatically.
- Verify payment through secure server-side callbacks or webhooks.
- Make payment operations idempotent.
- Never trust payment success based only on frontend response.
- Store booking price and commission snapshots.
- Preserve booking history.
- Do not delete confirmed bookings.

## 9.5 Payment Integration

Prepare a payment abstraction layer.

The system must support integration with payment providers suitable for Uzbekistan, such as Click or Payme, subject to merchant credentials and provider documentation.

If payment credentials are unavailable during development:

- Implement a payment interface and provider adapter.
- Use a clearly labeled development payment mode.
- Do not present simulated payments as real payments.
- Keep production payment configuration separate.

The project is considered complete when the following scenario works end to end:

A visitor opens the TourChef Landing Page.

They understand what TourChef is and see available culinary experiences.

They open the Reels section and watch videos of restaurants, chefs, and traditional Uzbek dishes.

They discover a video connected to a masterclass.

They click Book Now.

If they are not logged in, they are redirected to Login or Register without losing the selected masterclass.

After authentication, they select a session, choose the number of guests, and complete the booking process.

The payment is processed or handled through the configured development payment mode.

The booking appears in the Client Cabinet.

The restaurant sees the booking in its Restaurant Cabinet.

The restaurant can manage the session and mark attendance.

After the experience is completed, the client can leave a review.

The Admin Panel can manage the restaurant, masterclass, Reel, booking, and payment records.

All core actions must work with real backend APIs and PostgreSQL.

**Build TourChef as a complete, production-oriented culinary tourism marketplace with a beautiful public website, a functional Reels discovery experience, and fully integrated Client, Restaurant, and Admin Cabinets.**
